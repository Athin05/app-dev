#Harry Potter: Prisoner of Azkaban

*overview*

In the movie, Harry returns to Hogwarts for his third year and learns that escaped prisoner Sirius Black is said to be hunting him. As Dementors guard the school, Harry discovers the truth, that Sirius is actually his godfather and was falsely accused of betraying his parents. With help from Ron and Hermione, Harry uncovers the real traitor and learns more about his family’s past.
